About Us

Building Skilled Women for a Better Gujarat

Government Mahila Industrial Training Institute, Surendranagar is a premier women's vocational training institute under the Government of Gujarat. Our institute is dedicated to providing quality skill-based education to women.

We offer NCVT-affiliated courses in various trades, helping students gain nationally recognized certificates that open doors to employment and self-employment opportunities across Gujarat and India.
